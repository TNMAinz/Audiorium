# Feature status

Status vocabulary: **implemented and verified**, **implemented, not verified**,
**partially implemented**, **blocked**, **planned**. "Verified" means a test or a
gate exercises it and the result was observed, not that the code looks right.

Evidence at the time of writing: `cargo test` 294 passed, 8 ignored, `npx vitest
run` 542 passed in 53 files, `npx tsc --noEmit` clean, and the synth gates green
against the shipped 2.0.2 Windows build: `.unlazy/synth-verify.mjs` (the instrument
started on a real output, played notes, kept a patch, recorded a take, bounced it
through the rack and stopped), `.unlazy/synth-lab-verify.mjs` (the page fits at
1440×860 with nothing clipped or overlapping, a real mouse press on a sharp keeps
it in place, the output trace fills while a note sounds, and every panel opens),
`.unlazy/synth-matrix-verify.mjs` (the unison stack, the comb, the table cycle and
the matrix), `.unlazy/synth-import-verify.mjs` (a wavetable and a Scala tuning
imported from files the app did not ship, and the faceplate at five window sizes) and
`.unlazy/synth-output-verify.mjs` (the instrument plays to the output the Devices page
names, and moves to another one while it runs). The earlier gates (delivery, fit, master, processors) remain the
evidence for the pages they cover.

The page pulls the louder side down by the difference the render measured, never up, and resets both when the page is left or a new master is rendered. The export test renders the same tone twice and measures the error below about 500 Hz at under a quarter of the flat dither's power while the total error power rises — the noise was moved, not removed — asserts no sample lands more than four codes from the signal, and checks two renders of one file are byte-identical. The FLAC writer codes one 4096-sample frame at a time and patches `STREAMINFO` with the frame count, block/frame sizes and the sample MD5 when the stream ends; the container chooses the extension. Tests: the render test renders the same source to both containers and asserts the FLAC is smaller, carries the same tags and a byte-identical cover, and decodes through `player::decode` |

## Synth

The instrument is a second source with a clock of its own: it does not read a file,
and it does not share the player's stream. It plays through the same rack.

| Feature | Status | Evidence / limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two-oscillator voice engine, sixteen voices | Implemented and verified | `synth.rs`: PolyBLEP saw and square, triangle and sine, a sub square an octave down, noise, a filter in five models (state-variable low/band/high pass, ladder, comb) with envelope and key tracking, two ADSRs, two shared LFOs on pitch, filter or amplitude, glide, and voice stealing. Tests: A4 measures 440 Hz, an octave up is twice the frequency, a closed lowpass removes a high note, a released note frees its voice and falls silent, forty note-ons never exceed sixteen voices, the same take renders identical frames twice, a slow attack opens, a glide sweeps to the new note, a two-semitone bend lands at 493.9 Hz, and every built-in patch validates and renders finite samples |
| Unison: up to eight detuned copies per oscillator | Implemented and verified | Each oscillator's stack is detuned edge to edge in cents and spread across the image, divided by its own count's root so a stack is wider rather than louder. The filter runs once per channel, and the voice's own pan still places the stack. Tests: seven copies come out 0.5–2× the level of one, an octave of spread puts the copies across the image (the channels' difference rises fourfold), thirty cents of detune makes the level swing where a steady stack does not, and the patch validates; the native gate played an eight-copy stack on a real device at −21.3 dBFS |
| Modulation matrix: eleven sources, eight destinations, a curve on every route | Implemented and verified | `Mod` routes a source (either LFO, both envelopes, velocity, key, wheel, per-note pressure from MPE or channel aftertouch, and four performance macros) to a destination (pitch, cutoff, resonance, oscillator levels, sub, noise, pan) with an amount in the destination's own units — octaves for pitch and cutoff, a share of the parameter for the rest — and a curve (linear, exponential, logarithmic, S) that shapes the source while keeping its sign. Routes to one destination sum, a patch holds eight, and every route is validated. Tests: a velocity route at half amount lands a fifth up (622.3 Hz against 440), a route to cutoff closes a bright note, two routes to one level add up, macro 2 at full travel moves A4 an octave, and an exponential curve reads half velocity as a quarter where linear reads a half. The native gate saved two routes and played them through the comb |
| Wavetable oscillator, with import | Implemented and verified | A table is up to eight frames of harmonics, built once into a mip chain that halves the band with every halving of length, so a reading picks the longest copy its pitch can carry and does not alias; the morph walks the frames. Imported tables are appended after the shipped four, kept in `%APPDATA%\AUDIORIUM	ables.json` as harmonic levels and rebuilt at launch, and re-importing a name updates that table in place so a saved patch keeps its index. The audio thread reads one `Arc` snapshot a frame, never a lock.
